# Fictional operations policy — demonstration only

## Scope
This is an authored example policy for DataPilot's synthetic commerce demonstration. It is not a real company's policy, contract, or legal guidance. It has no authority outside this example workspace.

## Delivery investigation
For a late-delivery alert, an analyst should check the order timestamp, promised delivery window, carrier events, and data freshness. Compare the affected group with a suitable historical period before suggesting operational changes. Predictions identify records for review and do not authorize customer-facing decisions.

## Escalation example
In this fictional workflow, an analyst may draft an internal investigation when the evaluated late-delivery rate exceeds 15 percent for a complete week. This illustrative threshold is a policy assumption, not a measured result or a recommendation for a real business. The application does not send messages or change orders.

## Evidence and customer information
Every investigation should distinguish observed records, predicted outcomes, and assumptions. Cite the source document and calculation. Do not upload customer names, payment details, or other sensitive data to a public demonstration.
